Questions

The short, honest answers.

What happens when the trial ends?

New downloads and recordings pause. Everything you already saved stays on your computer and keeps playing. Enter a license key and you're back in business.

Does it work on every site?

Grabwave works with hundreds of sites, but not all of them. Some sites block automated downloads, and protected (DRM) video can't be saved. The engine is updated often, which fixes most breakages quickly.

Is it legal to use?

That depends on what you save and where you live. Only save content you own, that is in the public domain, or that you have permission or a legal right to keep for yourself, and respect the terms of the sites you use.

How do I get my key?

You receive it by email right after you pay. In Grabwave, open Help, choose Enter license key, and paste it in. Keys are checked on your computer, so no internet connection is needed to activate.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

One license is for one person on their own computers. If you replace your computer, just enter your key again.

Windows says "unrecognized app". Is it safe?

Windows shows that screen for new apps that haven't built up a download history yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. Only download Grabwave from this page or its official releases page.

What about refunds and support?

If Grabwave doesn't work for you, write to us within 14 days of buying for a refund. Write to support@example.com for help any time.
