Privacy policy

The short version

Grabwave runs on your computer. Your links, downloads and settings stay there. We have no accounts and do not track what you save.

What the app contacts

  • Update checks. The app asks GitHub for a small file that says whether a newer version exists, and downloads updates from there. GitHub receives your IP address as any website would.
  • The sites you choose. When you save something, the app connects to that site, just as a browser would.
  • Download engine updates. The app may fetch updates to its download components from their public sources.

License keys

License keys are checked on your computer. Activating a key does not send anything to us.

Purchases

Payments are handled by our payment provider, who is the seller of record. They collect your name, email and payment details under their own privacy policy. We receive your email address and order details so we can send your key and support you.

This website

This site does not use advertising trackers. If we add basic analytics, we will list it here.
