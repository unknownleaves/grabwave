How to record a live stream

A Grabwave guide

Live streams disappear when they end. If you have permission to keep one, such as your own broadcast, a public event or a talk, you can record it as it happens.

1. Open the stream page

Copy the address of the live page.

2. Paste it into Grabwave

Grabwave detects that it's live and starts recording to a normal video file.

3. Let it run

Recording continues until the stream ends. You can keep working; use Balanced or Light mode if your computer feels slow.

4. Auto-record

Add a channel to your watchlist and Grabwave records it when it goes live.

Good to know

  • Record only streams you have the right to keep, and follow the platform's terms.
  • Long recordings use lots of disk space, so keep an eye on free storage.
