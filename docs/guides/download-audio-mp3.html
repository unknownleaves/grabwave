How to save audio from a video as MP3

A Grabwave guide

Lectures, talks, podcasts and your own recordings are often easier to listen to than watch.

Steps

  1. Paste the link into Grabwave.
  2. Choose Audio only (MP3) as the quality.
  3. Press Enter. The MP3 lands on your Shelf.

Already have a video file? Open it from the Shelf and use the convert option to make an MP3.

Only save audio you have the right to keep.
