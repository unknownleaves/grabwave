How to save a video to watch offline

A Grabwave guide

Watching offline is handy on flights, commutes and slow connections. Here is how to do it with content you're allowed to keep: your own uploads, public-domain or Creative Commons videos, and anything the owner has permitted.

1. Copy the link

Open the video page and copy its address from the browser bar.

2. Paste it into Grabwave

Paste the link into the box and press Enter. Grabwave reads the page and shows the available qualities.

3. Pick a quality

Best quality makes the largest file. 720p is a good balance for phones. Audio only saves just the sound as MP3.

4. Watch it

The finished file appears on your Shelf. Open it in the built-in player or find it in your folder and copy it to a phone or tablet.

Good to know

  • Protected (DRM) video can't be saved. That's by design.
  • Check the site's terms and your local law before saving.
  • If a download fails, use Fix it on the failed item; Grabwave tries other routes automatically.
