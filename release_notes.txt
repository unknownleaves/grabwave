Grabwave 2.11
- Built-in video player: double-click a video on the Shelf to watch it inside Grabwave, including formats the browser tab can't play.
- More ways to save a video: if one method fails, Grabwave now tries the next (page video list, captured stream, ffmpeg copy, gallery-dl).
- Fixes pages that only gave a short preview clip instead of the full video.
- Safer updates: if a new version can't start, Grabwave puts the previous one back.
- Faster downloads in Maximum mode (16 pieces at once).
- The installer now closes Grabwave's background programs itself, so updates don't get stuck.
