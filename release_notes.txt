Grabwave 2.28

New
- Finished downloads show their picture height (for example 1080p) next to the size and length, and the log records how each file was fetched.
- Highest quality on sites that list their own resolutions: Grabwave reads that list and takes the best one directly, and records what the site offered in the log.
- Best quality: when a video is saved from the stream the page was playing, Grabwave now looks for the highest quality version of that stream first (the page's player often starts on a low one).
- More album and profile sites: pages that list albums are followed into each album, and every video in them is downloaded.
- Albums, profiles, channels and playlists: paste one and Grabwave finds every video on it and downloads them all, each as its own item in a folder named after the page. Long pages are followed page by page, and in the Browse tab the download button scrolls the page to the end first.
- Asks before adding a large batch, and skips videos that are already in your list or already saved.
- Downloads from one site run a few at a time with a short gap, and a busy site gets an automatic second try after a few minutes.
- Warns when disk space is low and ends recordings before the drive fills up.
- Help > Copy diagnostics puts the version details and recent log lines on your clipboard for support. Grabwave now keeps a log file in its settings folder.
- VR videos: the built-in player has a VR menu. Pick the layout (it is guessed from the file name and picture size) and a side-by-side video becomes one picture you drag to look around, scroll to zoom, and double-click to reset.
- Free trial and license keys: Grabwave runs free for 14 days. Help > Enter license key unlocks it for good.

Improved
- Watch opens live streams as well as saved videos, and tries more routes for video sites before giving up.
- Sites that block automatic live capture are captured in the background.
- The list saves itself every half minute.
