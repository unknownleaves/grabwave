Grabwave 2.18

New
- Profiles, channels and playlists: paste a Pornhub, xhamster, eporner or RedTube profile, channel or playlist page (and similar pages on many other sites) and Grabwave downloads every video on it.
- Albums and profiles: paste an erome album or profile, or a redgifs profile, and Grabwave finds every video and downloads them all, each as its own item in a folder named after the album or profile.
- Free trial and license keys. Grabwave now runs free for 14 days. Help > Enter license key unlocks it for good. The header shows how many trial days are left.
- When a trial ends, new downloads, recordings, Watch and Fix it pause. Your Shelf and the built-in player keep working.
- Watch button streams a video through a local relay so it plays in the built-in player.
- Sites that block live capture (error 418) are now captured automatically in the background.

Improved
- Watch now opens live rooms (Stripchat, XHamsterLive, Chaturbate) and tries more routes for YouTube and X before giving up.
- Built-in player handles H.264 video.
- Download engine and fallbacks are more resilient across sites.
